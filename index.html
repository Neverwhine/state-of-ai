JENSEN'S THREE SCALING LAWS

Each law creates a new performance ceiling — and a new investment thesis. GTC 2025–2026.

LAW 1

Pre-Training

More data, more parameters. The original curve — now plateauing as quality data runs scarce.

LAW 2

Post-Training / RL

RLHF, constitutional AI, reward modeling. The current frontier driving model alignment and capability.

LAW 3

Test-Time Compute

Reasoning at inference — chain-of-thought, thinking tokens. The emerging curve driving inference demand explosion.

Source: Jensen Huang, NVIDIA GTC 2025–2026 keynotes

Base text generation is commoditizing. But the Three Scaling Laws mean the frontier keeps moving — from pre-training to post-training to test-time compute. Value migrates to orchestration, inference optimization, and proprietary data.

As raw intelligence gets cheaper, the center of gravity shifts. Value moves from generating answers to getting work done.

THE VULNERABILITY PARADOX

You must make yourself vulnerable to extract value — but that will change.

Supply Chain

Snyk ToxicSkills: 37% of OpenClaw community skills contained flawed code. 200+ GitHub security advisories.

Prompt Injection

EchoLeak attack on browser agents. Slack AI data exfiltration. Gemini memory poisoning demonstrated.

Over-Permission

Agents need files, email, calendar, purchases to be useful. Every permission granted is an attack surface.

The Paradox

Today: accept risk to capture value. Tomorrow: agent-specific security layers, capability-based permissions, cryptographic identity.

THE ELECTRON GAP PARADOX

China generates 2× more electricity than the US and added 543 GW of new power capacity in 2024 alone — more than the US has built in its entire history. By 2030, China is projected to have ~400 GW of spare capacity, triple the power demand of the entire global data center fleet. Energy experts who visit China describe power availability as a "solved problem."

Yet the US consumes nearly 2× more data center electricity. The asymmetry: the US has the chips but is hitting energy bottlenecks (Morgan Stanley forecasts a 44 GW shortfall by 2028). China has the electrons but is constrained by US export controls on high-end GPUs.

The race for AI supremacy may not be won by who builds the best model — but by who solves their bottleneck first.

Source: Brookings (Feb 2026), Fortune, IEA, Morgan Stanley

The companies that ship fastest, iterate hardest, and stay closest to the user are the ones that convert resources into defensible advantage. Culture is not a perk — it is the conversion rate of everything above it.

↑ DISTRIBUTION (Roof)

A model without users is a science project. Distribution — through products, APIs, partnerships, developer ecosystems, and network effects — is what turns capability into revenue. OpenAI has ChatGPT. Meta has 3 billion users. Google has Search. The companies that own the interface own the margin.

Three pillars are necessary. Five layers are sufficient. DVC invests across the full building — with a bias toward the culture and distribution layers where durable value concentrates.

The five-layer thesis describes what it takes to win. But even with all five layers aligned, the fundamental economics of AI are still being figured out.
